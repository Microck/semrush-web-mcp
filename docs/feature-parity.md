# Useful SEO and GEO coverage

The MCP and CLI expose the same 48 authenticated read operations. All 48
passed live MCP calls on October 14, 2026. See the [complete catalog](tool-catalog.md)
for methods and inputs, and [verification](verification.md) for evidence.

## Covered research

| Workflow | Coverage |
| --- | --- |
| Keyword research | Metrics, questions, SERPs, ads, topics, paginated Keyword Magic, groups and totals |
| Organic research | Full rankings, pages, subdomains, position changes and competitors with provider totals |
| Historical/mobile research | Snapshot discovery, timestamp selection, daily/monthly reports and mobile databases |
| Keyword gaps | Organic overlaps, per-site totals and paginated gap records for two to five domains |
| Link building | Full backlinks, referring domains, anchors, indexed pages, outgoing domains, filters and pagination |
| Backlink comparisons | Competitor prospects, profile comparisons, history, authority, categories and country distribution |
| GEO research | Domain AI visibility, mentions, citation sources and country distribution |
| Existing account data | Projects, folders, saved keyword lists, audit campaigns and report permissions |

The original eleven overview tools were a subset. Organic rankings and
competitors now use full paginated reports. Anchors and indexed pages also
use full table reports. Keyword ideas and paid keyword/competitor overviews
remain explicitly labeled samples. Keyword Magic provides paginated keyword
research. Pagination preserves provider caps and never claims unlimited exports.

## Restrictions and remaining parity gaps

This is verified research coverage, not a claim that every Semrush web action
is implemented. Account permissions and configured projects limit what can
be exercised without changing account state:

- The account's full Advertising Toolkit view displays a subscription gate.
  Paid overview samples are accessible; full advertising/PLA workflows are
  not implemented or claimed as verified.
- The account reports no AI Visibility Toolkit entitlement. Domain AI reports
  work, but dedicated prompt research, brand monitoring, perception and AI
  competitor-gap workflows are not implemented or claimed as verified.
- The account reports a domain export allowance of zero. JSON output can be
  saved locally; this integration does not bypass paid web-export permissions.
- There are no Site Audit campaigns or configured websites in the inspected
  project folder. Detailed audit, rank-tracking, backlink-audit and on-page
  project reports remain unverified and are not exposed as working tools.
- Content generation, document editing, publishing, integrations, project
  creation, crawl execution and paid refreshes are not implemented. They
  change account state or consume separate generation/crawl allowances.

No subscription, trial, project, crawl or paid refresh was started to obtain
these results. Read-only account discovery explains unavailable data without
inventing empty research results. Billing, social publishing and storefront
apps are outside the SEO/GEO research scope.
